# Center Around Gray

Keep the color inside a circle and turn the rest of the image to grayscale. The edge of the circle is soft.

## Parameters

- **Radius** The radius of the colored circle, as a fraction of the image (0 to 1).
- **Center X** and **Center Y** The position of the circle, from 0 to 1. `0.5, 0.5` is the middle of the image.
